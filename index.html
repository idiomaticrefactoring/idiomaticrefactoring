About

简介

I am a Research Scientist at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computing, Australian National University (ANU), advised by Prof. Zhenchang Xing.

现任南洋理工大学（NTU）Research Scientist。于澳大利亚国立大学（ANU）计算机学院获博士学位，导师为邢臻创教授。

My research sits at the intersection of software engineering and AI, with a focus on code evolution, program comprehension, and software quality — from refactoring and migration and API evolution to mining software repositories, empirical studies, and LLM-driven automation.

研究聚焦软件工程与人工智能的交叉领域，关注代码演化、程序理解与软件质量，涵盖重构与迁移、API 演化、软件仓库挖掘、实证研究，以及基于大语言模型的自动化软件工程。

I have published 14+ peer-reviewed papers in top venues such as ICSE, FSE, and TSE, including 6+ first-author papers in Core A* / CCF-A venues. My work received the ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Award at ICSE 2024.

已在 ICSE、FSE、TSE 等顶级会议与期刊发表 14+ 篇论文，其中 6+ 篇为第一作者且发表于 CCF-A / Core A* venue。获 ICSE 2024 ACM SIGSOFT 杰出论文奖。

A defining theme of my work is translating research into usable tools. I developed the RIdiom IDE plugin, the RefactoringIdioms Python package, and the DeIdiom web platform.

研究工作的一贯主题是将成果落地为可用工具：RIdiom IDE 插件、RefactoringIdioms Python 包，以及 DeIdiom 在线平台。
