About

简介

I am an Assistant Professor in Software Engineering at Nanjing University. Before joining Nanjing University, I was a Research Scientist at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computing at the Australian National University (ANU), advised by Prof. Zhenchang Xing.

现任南京大学软件工程助理教授。此前任职于南洋理工大学（NTU），担任 Research Scientist。于澳大利亚国立大学（ANU）计算机学院获博士学位，导师为 邢振昌教授。

My research focuses on trustworthy intelligent software engineering, addressing how to make AI-assisted software development more understandable, reliable, and consistent. My work centers on three interconnected challenges: software understanding, enabling AI to reliably understand code and heterogeneous software artifacts; software quality assurance, ensuring the quality and reliability of AI-generated and AI-assisted software through analysis, refactoring, and optimization; and cross-artifact consistency, maintaining semantic alignment among code, documentation, configurations, APIs, and other software artifacts as software evolves.

I have published papers in top software engineering venues, including ICSE, FSE, and TSE. My work received the ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Award at ICSE 2024.

在软件工程领域顶级会议和期刊 ICSE、FSE、TSE 等发表多篇论文， 相关研究获 ICSE 2024 ACM SIGSOFT 杰出论文奖

A defining theme of my research is bridging research and practice through usable tools. I developed the RIdiom IDE plugin, the RefactoringIdioms Python package, and the DeIdiom web platform, bringing research ideas into practical software development tools.

研究注重推动学术研究与实际应用的衔接，致力于将研究成果转化为可用的工具。 已开发 RIdiom IDE 插件、RefactoringIdioms Python 包，以及 DeIdiom 在线平台，将研究成果转化为支持实际软件开发的工具。

欢迎本科生、硕士生与博士生加入。请将简历发送至 zejun.zhang@nju.edu.cn。
